### SignetCRM ([signetcrm.com](https://signetcrm.com))

Shop management software for repair businesses. It went live in September 2026.

- Each shop gets its own database and branding, all running on one codebase
- Estimates, invoices and online booking, with links customers can approve from their phone
- A workflow board that follows every job from estimate to paid
- An AI front desk that answers the shop's phone calls
- Card payments, staff time clocks and automated tests

Built with PHP, MariaDB, Node.js, Gemini and Telnyx.

### Lotline

Predicts what a vehicle will sell for at salvage auctions like Copart and IAA.

- Collected about 80,000 auction lots and 960,000 photos, plus condition reports and sale details
- The price model hit 18% median error on its first run
- Has a web dashboard for browsing lots and training models

Built with Python, pandas, scikit-learn, LightGBM, PyTorch, PHP and MariaDB.
